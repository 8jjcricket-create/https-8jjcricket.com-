# Venkatesh Iyer Finally Gets His Chance at RCB and Makes It Count

Venkatesh Iyer made the most of his long-awaited opportunity with Royal Challengers Bengaluru by producing one of his most impactful performances of IPL 2026.

After spending much of the season waiting for a consistent role, the left-handed all-rounder stepped into the spotlight and immediately delivered under pressure. His aggressive batting and confident approach provided RCB with crucial momentum during a vital stage of the tournament.

Iyer’s innings showcased the qualities that once made him one of the most exciting emerging players in Indian T20 cricket. His clean striking, ability to attack spin, and fearless mindset helped Bengaluru maintain control during a high-pressure encounter.

The performance also highlighted RCB’s growing squad depth as the franchise continues preparing for the playoff stage. Contributions from players outside the established core could become extremely important as the tournament moves toward the knockout rounds.

With confidence now building around his role within the team, Venkatesh Iyer could become an important asset for Bengaluru during the closing phase of IPL 2026.
