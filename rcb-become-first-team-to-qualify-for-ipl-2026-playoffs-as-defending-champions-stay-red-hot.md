# RCB Become First Team to Qualify for IPL 2026 Playoffs

Royal Challengers Bengaluru have officially become the first franchise to secure qualification for the IPL 2026 playoffs after another commanding performance.

RCB’s consistency throughout the season has transformed them into one of the strongest teams in the competition. Led by Virat Kohli’s remarkable batting form and a disciplined bowling attack, Bengaluru have successfully maintained momentum during the most pressure-filled phase of the league stage.

The defending champions have balanced aggressive batting with improved execution in key moments, allowing them to dominate several high-pressure fixtures. Contributions from both experienced stars and emerging players have strengthened RCB’s position as serious title contenders.

Their early playoff qualification also highlights the tactical stability and squad depth developed throughout IPL 2026. Fans are now beginning to believe that this could finally become one of the franchise’s most successful campaigns in recent years.

With the knockout stage approaching, Royal Challengers Bengaluru will now focus on securing a top-two finish and carrying their momentum into the playoffs.
