# Hardik Pandya Injury Update: MI Captain Doubtful for PBKS Clash

Mumbai Indians are facing uncertainty ahead of their IPL 2026 clash against Punjab Kings, with captain Hardik Pandya still struggling with injury concerns.

The all-rounder has already missed recent matches due to fitness issues, and reports suggest the franchise is continuing to assess his condition carefully before making a final decision. His absence would represent a major setback for Mumbai, particularly given his importance as both a batter and seam-bowling option.

Hardik’s leadership and experience have remained central to Mumbai Indians throughout the tournament, even during a difficult campaign. Fans are now waiting for official confirmation regarding his availability for the important Dharamsala fixture.

In recent training sessions, Pandya was reportedly seen practicing separately from the main squad, increasing speculation about his readiness for match action. The franchise management has remained cautious while monitoring his recovery process.

With IPL 2026 entering a critical phase, Mumbai Indians will hope their captain can return fully fit before the season concludes.
